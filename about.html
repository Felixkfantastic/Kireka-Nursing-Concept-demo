Our approach

Classroom instruction, matched with real hospital exposure.

Every KINUMITS student trains toward a two-and-a-half-year certificate or diploma, combining structured coursework with supervised clinical rotations at China-Uganda Friendship Hospital, Naguru — so graduates leave with practice, not just theory.

LIC

Licensed programmes

UHPAB-licensed and listed on the Ministry of Education's register of health training institutions.

HOS

Hospital-based placement

Clinical rotations at a real partner hospital, tracked through a placement logbook.

INT

Two intakes a year

New students can join in January/February or July/August.
