Getting ready

What to bring when you join.

Beyond tuition, students are responsible for personal boarding items and a share of shared classroom/office supplies each semester.

KIT

Personal items

  • Mattress, blanket, bedsheets
  • Washing & bathing soap, towel
  • 20-litre jerrycan, mosquito net
  • Plastic basin/bucket
  • Knife (girls)
  • 2 pairs black/brown leather laced low-heel closed shoes
  • Sports shoes
  • 2 black skirts + white tops (girls) or 2 black trousers + white t-shirts (boys)
  • Stationery — 6 exercise books, 10 counter books, pens
MED

Medical kit & class supplies

  • Clinical thermometer
  • Stethoscope
  • Blood pressure machine
  • 1 pair white gumboots (midwifery students)
  • Uniform (purchased at school)
  • Set course textbook — see below

Nursing students carry the Nursing and Midwifery Procedure Manual. Midwifery students carry Human Anatomy and Physiology for Nursing and Allied Sciences by Anand.
