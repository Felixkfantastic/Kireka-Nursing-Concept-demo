Home/Courses

Four programmes. One entry standard. Real clinical time.

Every track runs two and a half years and combines classroom instruction with supervised hospital placement.

EN
Certificate · 2.5 Years

Enrolled Nursing

Foundational patient care — vitals, wound care, medication administration, ward procedure — building toward independent nursing practice.

  • Anatomy & physiology fundamentals
  • Clinical nursing procedure
  • Supervised ward rotations
  • Placement logbook tracked each semester
MW
Certificate · 2.5 Years

Midwifery

Maternal and newborn care across antenatal, delivery, and postnatal stages, with hands-on labour ward exposure.

  • Antenatal & postnatal care
  • Safe delivery practice
  • Newborn care fundamentals
  • Hospital-based clinical rotations
GC
Certificate · 2.5 Years

Geriatric Care

Specialised training for Uganda's growing elderly-care sector — chronic condition management, mobility support, and home-based care skills.

  • Chronic illness management
  • Mobility & daily-living support
  • Patient communication & dignity in care
SN
Certificate & Diploma · 2.5 Years

Sonography

Diagnostic ultrasound imaging training at certificate or diploma level, preparing students for radiology and diagnostic support roles.

  • Ultrasound imaging fundamentals
  • Diagnostic technique & equipment handling
  • Clinical imaging placement
Entry requirements

What you need to apply.

AGE

Age & academic

Applicants must be 18 years or older, with a pass in Mathematics, English, Biology, Chemistry and Physics — a pass under the old curriculum, or minimum grade “D” under the new curriculum.

DOC

At interview

Bring original + photocopy of academic documents, a photocopy of your O-Level ID, 6 recent coloured passport photos (name on back), and the UGX 50,000 admission fee.
